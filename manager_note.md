# Q3 Application Funnel Review (July–September 2026)

Cleaning the July–September 2026 file removed 6 exact duplicates and standardised 9 work_mode labels (1,200 unique applications; 15 missing-source rows are excluded only from source rankings).

1. Best hiring source: Referrals convert best overall at 10.7 hires per 100 applications (13/121), ahead of email (8.1) and search (5.7; highest volume at 25 hires). This does not hold by role: referrals lead only in Design and Marketing, email leads Software, search leads Data and Finance, and college leads Operations.

2. Completing all 4 steps: Applicants finishing all 4 steps are shortlisted 5.2× more often—48.4% (195/403) vs. 9.3% (74/797) for 0–3 steps, a 39.1 percentage-point lift across all roles.

3. Paid vs. unpaid replies: Paid roles reply 4 days faster at the median (3 vs. 7 days; mean 3.2 vs. 7.6) and leave 13.1% without a reply vs. 29.6% for unpaid (+16.5 pp). With unpaid roles representing 38.8% of applications, this gap warrants action.

Recommendation for next month: Send automated 7-day reply nudges to unpaid employers and display each listing's typical response time.

Data caveats: Eleven 0-step applicants were shortlisted (3 hired), suggesting a step-tracking bug; role-by-source samples are small (64 hires total, 1 in Finance); and some late-September blanks may still be pending.
